Challenge: Reimagine Learning at RUET
Build the next generation of learning platforms for RUET students.
RUET students rely on multiple sources for their academic life: course materials, class notes, assignments, notices, question banks, exam preparation, and communication with classmates and teachers.
The challenge is to build an AI-powered platform that makes the academic experience at RUET simpler, smarter, and more personalized.
Your solution could rethink how students:
Access course materials
Organize lecture notes
Prepare for exams
Practice questions
Track assignments and deadlines
Discover useful resources
Ask questions about course content
Learn difficult concepts
Collaborate with classmates
Get personalized academic recommendations
AI is the differentiator
Don't just build another LMS.
Think about:
What could an LMS do if AI was built into its core?
For example:
AI Course Assistant
Upload course materials → ask questions → get answers grounded in the materials.
Smart Exam Preparation
Upload lecture slides → generate a study plan → practice questions → identify weak topics.
AI Assignment Assistant
Assignment posted → student receives explanations, hints, and personalized guidance without simply getting the answer.

Course Knowledge Base
All course materials become searchable through a conversational AI interface.
Personalized Dashboard
The system analyzes courses, deadlines, performance, and study activity to recommend what the student should focus on next.
These are examples only. Participants are free to build their own interpretation.
Challenge requirement
Your project should:
Address a real problem in the academic experience.
Include a working prototype.
Use AI meaningfully.
Demonstrate how the solution could benefit RUET students.
Suggested technologies
Participants are encouraged to use:
Gemini API
Google AI Studio
Google Antigravity
Google Cloud
Cloud Run
Other technologies are allowed.

The goal
You don't need to build a complete LMS in three hours.
Pick one frustrating part of student life and rethink it with AI.
